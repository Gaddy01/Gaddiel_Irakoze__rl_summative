# Scientific results — evidence-based discussion draft

**Usage:** adapt these notes to the assignment report. Never present simulation scores as realized economic returns.

## Experimental design and framework

Four native TorchRL methods (DQN, REINFORCE, PPO, A2C) were each evaluated using ten predeclared hyperparameter configurations, with 1,000 complete 24-step episodes per configuration (40,000 training episodes, 960,000 simulator interactions). All models used the same 53-observation, 17-action investment simulator. A fixed training seed (42) was used for every configuration. Historical validation (2016–2023 trade-signal initialization, simulator seeds 50000–50099) determined one model per method; the 2024 holdout was assessed only afterward.

## Rewards and leading methods

The selected PPO model (ppo_03) achieved the highest held-out mean composite reward (0.3720, SD 0.1206) and mean final hypothetical net worth (130.92 from an initial 100 units). DQN (dqn_09) achieved 0.3551; the Rule-Based baseline achieved 0.2408. The PPO advantage over this baseline was 0.1312 reward units. These are simulated outcomes conditional on the frozen policies and 2024 starting signals, not independent observations of actual investment outcomes.

## Hyperparameter robustness

Mean historical validation reward across the 10 configurations was DQN 0.3147, REINFORCE 0.2450, PPO 0.3559, A2C 0.2278. This is descriptive robustness to the *tested* hyperparameters, not an estimate of uncertainty across independent training seeds. Some configurations changed multiple parameters simultaneously, limiting causal attribution.

## Reward learning and convergence/stability

- **DQN (dqn_09)**: average training reward changed from -0.0229 (episodes 1–100) to +0.2422 (901–1000). The predeclared near-final tolerance was first maintained from episode 401. This diagnostic is not a mathematical convergence proof.

- **REINFORCE (reinforce_03)**: average training reward changed from -0.0382 (episodes 1–100) to +0.2621 (901–1000). The predeclared near-final tolerance was first maintained from episode 501. This diagnostic is not a mathematical convergence proof.

- **PPO (ppo_03)**: average training reward changed from +0.1543 (episodes 1–100) to +0.3548 (901–1000). The predeclared near-final tolerance was first maintained from episode 201. This diagnostic is not a mathematical convergence proof.

- **A2C (a2c_01)**: average training reward changed from -0.0392 (episodes 1–100) to +0.2966 (901–1000). The predeclared sustained near-final tolerance was not detected. This diagnostic is not a mathematical convergence proof.

## Losses and exploration

- **REINFORCE**: first-100 mean entropy 1.0435 nats; final-100 mean 0.6750 nats. Entropy is an exploration/concentration diagnostic, not itself a reward metric.

- **PPO**: first-100 mean entropy 0.8560 nats; final-100 mean 0.0267 nats. Entropy is an exploration/concentration diagnostic, not itself a reward metric.

- **A2C**: first-100 mean entropy 1.0462 nats; final-100 mean 0.3871 nats. Entropy is an exploration/concentration diagnostic, not itself a reward metric.

DQN uses Huber temporal-difference loss with TorchRL replay/target updates; its first ten missing episode-loss values arise from replay warmup. Policy-gradient actor losses, critic losses and entropy bonuses have different mathematical scales and must not be compared numerically to DQN loss.

## Generalization and statistical uncertainty

- DQN: historical mean 0.3524 → held-out 2024 mean 0.3551; shift +0.0027.

- REINFORCE: historical mean 0.3393 → held-out 2024 mean 0.3434; shift +0.0040.

- PPO: historical mean 0.3666 → held-out 2024 mean 0.3720; shift +0.0054.

- A2C: historical mean 0.3393 → held-out 2024 mean 0.3434; shift +0.0040.

Seed-paired exploratory bootstrap: PPO − DQN mean reward +0.0169, 95% conditional bootstrap interval [+0.0094, +0.0244] over 100 simulation seeds. This does not account for variation from retraining.

## Policy behavior

- DQN: Hold 1630, Study 254, Pilot 254, Expand 252, Exit 10 decisions in 2,400 held-out steps.

- REINFORCE: Hold 1800, Study 200, Pilot 200, Expand 200, Exit 0 decisions in 2,400 held-out steps.

- PPO: Hold 1502, Study 300, Pilot 300, Expand 298, Exit 0 decisions in 2,400 held-out steps.

- A2C: Hold 1800, Study 200, Pilot 200, Expand 200, Exit 0 decisions in 2,400 held-out steps.

The 2024 economic proxy values for construction materials and medicaments are high, but the import scores are not observed returns on real factories. Portfolio and development outcomes are generated by the synthetic simulator.

## Limitations and next work

Only one trained policy initialization/seed was used per hyperparameter configuration. The holdout spans 100 random simulator initializations **but only one 2024 real trade-signal year**; their returns may be dependent through shared initial-year proxies. UN Comtrade preview flags are `source_is_reported=False` in the input records, with HS revision variation; construction demand is clipped above the training range. Observed imports do not validate downstream firm viability, capital return or employment. Do not assert statistically general superior algorithms without accounting for training seeds and multiple comparisons. Produce a separate interactive environment visualization and full-screen recorded demonstration as required by the assignment.
